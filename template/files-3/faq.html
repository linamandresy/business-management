Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00pm and check-out is until 12:00pm.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes, breakfast is complimentary for all guests.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, please indicate your flight details when booking or contact us directly.

Is there parking at the hotel?

Yes, we offer secure on-site parking for guests.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed except for service animals.
